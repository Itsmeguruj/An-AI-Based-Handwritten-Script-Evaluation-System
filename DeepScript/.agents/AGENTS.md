# Project Rules - DeepScript

## Eye-Soothing Colors and Glows
- All colors and glows in the interface must be highly soothing to the eyes to prevent eye strain.
- Avoid high-intensity, bright neon glow shadows or excessive glowing backdrops. Keep contrast smooth and comfortable.
- Ensure all style changes, color updates, and layout properties remain fully compatible with both dark and white (light) themes, maintaining readable text and balanced contrast.
